Our Car Finder

Get new listings on your phone

A few times a day the finder checks the car sites. When it finds a new car or a price drop, it can send a notification to your phone through ntfy, a free app. You don't need an account or to give out a phone number or email.

Set it up (about 2 minutes)

  1. Get the topic name from Pete. Notifications go to a private "topic" with a random name. It isn't written on this page because anyone who knows it can see the alerts, so ask for it by text or in person.
  2. Install the ntfy app.
    iPhone: App Store ↗ Android: Google Play ↗
  3. Allow notifications. When the app asks, tap Allow. If you skipped it, turn them on in your phone's Settings → Notifications → ntfy.
  4. Subscribe to the topic.
    • Tap the + button.
    • Type the topic name exactly as you got it. It's case-sensitive, so copying and pasting is safest.
    • Leave the server as ntfy.sh.
    • Tap Subscribe.
  5. Done. The topic shows up in the app's list. Older notifications from the last 12 hours or so appear right away; new ones arrive as the finder runs.

What you'll get

Great deal
A car priced well under what similar cars sell for. Highest priority, so it may make a louder sound.
New match
A new car in the budget, year, mileage and distance limits.
New near-miss
A car slightly outside the budget. Near misses (a little over budget) come through too, at a lower priority.
Price drop
A car you've already been told about got cheaper. Shows the old and new price.
N more new listings
When lots of cars show up at once, the extras come as one short list.

Each alert has a photo, the price, mileage, where the car is and how far away. Tap the notification to open the listing on the car site, or tap Open report to come back to this page and see everything side by side.

Tips

  • Too many alerts? In ntfy, tap the topic, then the bell or ⋮ menu, to mute it for a while or change the sound. You can also long-press a notification on your phone to make it silent.
  • Not getting any? Check the topic name is spelled exactly right and that notifications are allowed for ntfy. On Android, also set the ntfy app's battery use to Unrestricted so the phone doesn't put it to sleep.
  • Quiet days are normal. No alert just means nothing new matched. This page always shows the current list.
  • Be careful with deals that look too good. Very cheap private listings, especially on Facebook, can be scams or down-payment ads. See the car and the title before paying anything.
  • Want to stop? Long-press (Android) or swipe left on (iPhone) the topic in ntfy and unsubscribe, or just delete the app.
