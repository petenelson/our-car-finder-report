Toyota Sienna

Minivan · 12 current matches

The roomiest van here and the only one with AWD. 2013–2014 drew far fewer complaints than 2011.

3rd generation 2011–2020

12 matches · $6,500–$9,995 · median 128k mi · 6 good/great deals
Powertrain
3.5L V6, 266 hp, 6-speed automatic (2011–2016). 2011–2012 base models also offered a 2.7L 4-cylinder (187 hp). 2017+ V6 makes 296 hp with an 8-speed.
Drive
FWD; AWD available with the V6 (the only AWD minivan of this era).
Features
7 or 8 seats. Power sliding doors on most trims; SE trim (2011+) has sport-tuned suspension. XLE/Limited add leather, sunroof, and power liftgate; Limited adds a dual-view rear entertainment screen option.
Updates
2015 refresh: new dashboard and gauges, quieter cabin, more standard features. 2018: Toyota Safety Sense standard.
Cargo
150.0 cu ft max · 87.1 behind 2nd row · 39.1 behind 3rd row · seats 7–8
Known issues
Power sliding door cables and motors are the usual repair. 2011, the first year of this generation, drew the most complaints.
Before you buy
Run both power sliding doors through several cycles; listen for grinding and check that they latch. Ask whether the sliding-door recall was done (VIN check at nhtsa.gov).

Honda Odyssey

Minivan · 38 current matches

The most listings in the search. It drives better than the Sienna, but budget for VCM issues and the timing belt.

4th generation 2011–2017

38 matches · $6,300–$9,999 · median 136k mi · 12 good/great deals
Powertrain
3.5L V6 with cylinder deactivation (VCM), 248 hp. 5-speed automatic on most 2011–2013 trims (6-speed on Touring); 6-speed on all trims from 2014.
Drive
FWD only.
Features
7 or 8 seats. EX and up get power sliding doors; Touring adds navigation, memory seats, and a 6-speed. 2014 Touring Elite has a built-in vacuum (HondaVAC).
Updates
2014 refresh: direct-injected V6, 6-speed standard, new front end and dash, LaneWatch side camera on EX and up, forward collision warning on upper trims.
Cargo
148.5 cu ft max · 93.1 behind 2nd row · 38.4 behind 3rd row · seats 7–8
Known issues
The V6's cylinder deactivation (VCM) is linked to oil burning and fouled spark plugs. Check for misfires and oil use.
Before you buy
Look for misfire codes and oil use on 2011–2013 (VCM). Ask for timing-belt service records past ~105k miles. Test-drive for shudder at 20–40 mph on 2014+.
