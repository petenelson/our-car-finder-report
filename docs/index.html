Cargo space by model

Manufacturer figures in cubic feet for the generations in range. "Max" is behind the front seats with the rear seats folded or removed. The bar on each card is scaled to 160 cu ft, and the tick marks space behind the 2nd row.

How deals are rated

Each run records the asking price, year and mileage of every listing of these models, including a wider $5k–$16k sample from Cars.com and all of CarMax. A price curve per model, fitted to the last 45 days, gives the expected price for a car's year and mileage. Autotrader listings use Kelley Blue Book's fair purchase price instead. Great deal means at least 15% under expected (or $1,500 under and 7.5% under). Good deal means at least 7% under (or $700 and 3.5%). More than 40% under is marked Too good to be true?: on Facebook especially, that is usually a down-payment ad or a scam. A listing that mentions title or mechanical trouble tops out at Good. Unusually cheap private listings can be scams, so see the car and the title before paying anything.
